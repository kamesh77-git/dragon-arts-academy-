## What is Bharatanatyam?

[Bharatanatyam](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Bharatanatyam) is a classical dance form from Tamil Nadu. It combines rhythmic footwork, sculptural poses, hand gestures and facial expression. It is one of the best known Indian classical dances and is performed around the world.

## Bharatanatyam for beginners: 5 first steps

### Step 1: Aramandi, the basic posture

Aramandi is a half-sitting position with knees bent outwards and heels together. It gives Bharatanatyam its strong, grounded look. Beginners build the leg strength to hold it comfortably.

### Step 2: Adavus, the building blocks

Adavus are short units of footwork, posture and arm movement. Students learn them in sets, such as tattadavu (stamping steps) and nattadavu (stretching steps). Every dance item is built from adavus.

### Step 3: Mudras, the hand gestures

Mudras (hastas) are hand gestures with meaning. Single-hand gestures include Pataka (flat hand) and Tripataka. Double-hand gestures combine both hands. Mudras let a dancer show a flower, a bird, the moon or a god.

### Step 4: Tala, keeping rhythm

Rhythm is the heartbeat of Bharatanatyam. Teachers use a wooden stick and block, or recited syllables, to keep time. Beginners learn to match their steps to the beat at different speeds.

### Step 5: Abhinaya, expression

Abhinaya is the art of expression. Dancers use the face, eyes and body to show emotions and tell stories. Beginners start with simple expressions before learning full story pieces.

## A beginner's learning path

| Stage | What students learn |
| --- | --- |
| First months | Aramandi, basic adavus, simple mudras |
| Building up | More adavu sets, speed changes, rhythm |
| Storytelling | Abhinaya, short dance items |
| Performing | Group items at events, then solo pieces |
| Advanced | Longer repertoire, possibly an arangetram |

Every student moves at their own pace. In our experience, regular practice matters more than age in Bharatanatyam for beginners.

## What to wear and bring

Bharatanatyam for beginners needs very little equipment:

- Comfortable clothes that allow free leg movement.
- Hair tied back neatly.
- A water bottle.
- Later, a practice saree or costume, as your teacher advises.

## How to practise at home

- Practise adavus slowly before trying them fast.
- Use a mirror to check posture.
- Keep sessions short, around 15 to 20 minutes.
- Rest if knees or ankles feel sore.
- Revise mudras while watching a performance.

## Benefits of Bharatanatyam for beginners and children

- **Fitness:** strong legs, good stamina and posture.
- **Memory:** sequences, rhythms and gestures train recall.
- **Confidence:** stage performances build self-belief.
- **Culture:** students connect with Tamil and Indian heritage.
- **Discipline:** steady practice teaches patience and focus.

Bharatanatyam for beginners rests on five foundations: aramandi, adavus, mudras, tala and abhinaya. Learn them step by step, practise a little every day, and performances will follow.

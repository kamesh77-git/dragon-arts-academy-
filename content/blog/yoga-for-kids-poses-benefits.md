## Key takeaways

- Yoga for kids builds flexibility, balance, posture and focus.
- Breathing exercises help children calm down when upset.
- Six easy poses are a great place to start at home.
- Keep practice short, playful and pain-free.
- A qualified teacher makes progress safer and faster.

## What is yoga for kids?

[Yoga](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Yoga) is an Indian practice that combines postures (asanas), breathing (pranayama) and relaxation. Yoga for kids uses the same building blocks, with simpler poses, shorter holds and more games.

Children often learn poses named after animals and nature. This makes practice easy to remember and fun to share.

## 6 benefits of yoga for kids

1. **Flexibility.** Gentle stretches keep muscles and joints supple.
2. **Balance and coordination.** Standing poses train steadiness.
3. **Better posture.** Yoga strengthens the back and core, useful for children who sit for long hours.
4. **Focus.** Holding a pose asks children to concentrate on one thing.
5. **Calm.** Slow breathing helps children settle when they feel worried or overexcited.
6. **Healthy habits.** Regular practice builds a routine that can last a lifetime.

The [World Health Organization](https://www.who.int/news-room/fact-sheets/detail/physical-activity) recommends that children aged 5 to 17 include activities that strengthen muscle and bone. Many yoga poses do exactly that.

## 6 easy yoga poses for kids

| Pose | Sanskrit name | What it helps |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Mountain | Tadasana | Posture and body awareness |
| Tree | Vrikshasana | Balance and focus |
| Cat-Cow | Marjaryasana-Bitilasana | Spine flexibility |
| Butterfly | Baddha Konasana | Hip and leg flexibility |
| Cobra | Bhujangasana | Back strength |
| Child's Pose | Balasana | Rest and calm |

### Mountain Pose (Tadasana)

Stand tall with feet together and arms by the sides. Imagine a string pulling the head up. Breathe slowly for five breaths.

### Tree Pose (Vrikshasana)

From Mountain, place one foot on the opposite ankle or calf, never on the knee. Bring palms together. Hold, then switch sides. Children love seeing how long their "tree" can stand.

### Cat-Cow

On hands and knees, round the back like a cat, then gently dip it like a cow. Move slowly with the breath.

### Butterfly Pose (Baddha Konasana)

Sit with the soles of the feet together and knees out to the sides. Gently flap the knees like wings.

### Cobra Pose (Bhujangasana)

Lie on the tummy with hands under the shoulders. Lift the chest gently, keeping elbows soft. Hiss like a snake for fun.

### Child's Pose (Balasana)

Kneel, sit back on the heels and fold forward with arms stretched out or by the sides. This is the perfect pose to end a session.

## Safety tips for parents

- Practise on a mat or soft carpet, away from furniture.
- Never push a child deeper into a stretch.
- Stop if anything hurts.
- Avoid practice right after a big meal.
- Learn new or harder poses with a qualified teacher.

## Making yoga fun at home

- Turn poses into a story: a tree in the wind, a snake in the grass.
- Practise together as a family for 10 minutes.
- Use a small reward chart for regular practice.
- End with one minute of quiet breathing in Child's Pose.
